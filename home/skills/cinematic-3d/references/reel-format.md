# The reel format

Source: a 21.6 s, 30 fps reel by @hbaffi ("POV: Claude does your 3D",
2026-11). It was analysed frame by frame and transcribed on 2026-11-08. His
inner film counter reads `/ 0723` (24.1 s at 30 fps). He played the film on
his MacBook, filmed it at his desk, and laid a crossed-out Blender logo, the
Claude icon and a caption over it. The caption reads "Comment 'skill' and it's
in your DMs" (a comment-to-DM funnel: 781 comments against 472 likes).

## Why it works

- The hook denies the obvious reading ("This isn't footage") over a frame that
  looks like footage, then proves it with the wire half on the same frame.
- The contrast shot gives the viewer a reason to want the thing: the plain
  prompt result, then the same shot with the skill.
- The four one-word pillars are a list a viewer can repeat. Each gets its own
  shot and its own visual proof.
- The CTA is a deliverable, the file, behind a comment, so the comment count
  feeds the ranking and the DMs build a list.

## Adapting it

Keep the order: denial hook, proof, contrast, method in named pillars, number,
CTA. Change the set (any one room or object with a motivated key), the
pillars and the deliverable. For a phone app, the set could be a phone on a
nightstand at 01:57, lit only by its screen, with the lock and stake mechanic
as the pillars. Follow the reel studio's rules for captions, safe zones and
the words the voice never says.
